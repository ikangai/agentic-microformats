Understanding Your Site's LLMs.txt File

Last updated: March 2026 3 minute read

What is an LLMs.txt file?

An LLMs.txt file is a plain text file that helps AI systems better understand and navigate your website. Similar to how a sitemap helps search engines index your content, an LLMs.txt file provides AI assistants and large language models (LLMs) with a structured overview of your site's content and capabilities.

The file is placed at the root of your website (e.g., https://yoursite.com/llms.txt) and written in Markdown format.

Why does it matter?

As AI assistants become more common, users increasingly ask them to help with tasks on websites — finding products, understanding services, or navigating content. An LLMs.txt file helps these AI assistants:

  • Understand what your site does and who it's for
  • Find the most important pages and documentation
  • Answer questions about your business more accurately
  • Provide better assistance to your visitors

How Wix handles LLMs.txt automatically

Wix automatically generates an LLMs.txt file for your site based on your site's content and structure. You don't need to create or manage this file manually.

The automatically generated file includes:

  • Your site name and a brief description
  • Links to your main pages
  • Links to your blog posts (if applicable)
  • Links to your store products (if applicable)

Customizing your LLMs.txt file

You can customize the automatically generated file from the Wix dashboard:

  1. Go to Settings in your Wix dashboard.
  2. Select SEO Tools.
  3. Click LLMs.txt.
  4. Edit the content as needed and click Save.

Frequently asked questions

Is LLMs.txt required for my site?

No, it's optional. However, having one can improve how AI assistants describe and interact with your site.

Does LLMs.txt affect my SEO?

LLMs.txt is designed for AI assistants, not traditional search engines. It has no direct impact on your search engine rankings.

Can I see my site's LLMs.txt file?

Yes, you can view it by navigating to https://yoursite.com/llms.txt in your browser.
